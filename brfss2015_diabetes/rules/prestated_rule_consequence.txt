P9 CONSEQUENCE LAYER - PRE-STATED RULE (written before any model is fitted)
Paper: P9, construction audit of two BRFSS-derived benchmark files (diabetes 2015; heart 2020/2022).
Question: how much do the construction defects change what a typical user of the file would conclude?
Scope: unweighted analyses only (that is how users use the files). No survey-weighted analysis
(the weighted fairness precision result in i336deep/data_note_plan_i336.md is NOT redone).
Descriptive and predictive only; nothing below is a causal claim.

======================================================================
COMMON SETTINGS
======================================================================
Python: python -I -X utf8, below-normal priority, n_jobs <= 4, at most 2 heavy processes.
Seeds: s in {20261125, 20261126, 20261127, 20261128, 20261129} (5 seeds).
Split: per seed, stratified 70/30 train/test (sklearn train_test_split, random_state = s).
  Diabetes: stratified on Diabetes_012 (3 classes), so prediabetes is spread evenly.
  Heart 2022: stratified on the 4-level combination of HadHeartAttack x HadAngina.
  Heart 2020: stratified on HeartDisease.
Models (exactly two families, "default-ish" settings as most papers use them):
  LR   = LogisticRegression(C=1.0, solver='lbfgs', max_iter=3000) after StandardScaler on numeric
         columns; categorical columns (heart files) one-hot encoded (handle_unknown='ignore').
         Diabetes file: all 21 columns treated as numeric (as distributed), standardized.
  LGBM = lightgbm.LGBMClassifier(n_estimators=100, learning_rate=0.1, num_leaves=31,
         random_state=s, n_jobs=4, verbose=-1); categorical columns ordinal-encoded to integer codes.
Metrics:
  AUC (ROC AUC); calibration-in-the-large CITL = mean predicted probability - observed event rate
  on the test set; sensitivity = share of a group flagged positive.
Thresholds: (t1) 0.5; (t2) "prevalence threshold" = the (1 - p) quantile of the model's predicted
  probabilities on its own TRAINING set, p = training prevalence of the label the model was fitted on
  (so the share flagged equals the file prevalence).
Feature importance: sklearn permutation_importance on the fitted pipeline with raw (pre-encoding)
  columns permuted, scoring='roc_auc', n_repeats=5, random_state=s, on a random subsample of
  30,000 test rows (seed s). Ranking by mean importance (ties broken by column order).
  Summaries: top-10 overlap (|top10_X intersect top10_Y|) and Kendall tau-b over all features.
Uncertainty / noise floor:
  - 5-seed results: report median and min-max range.
  - Seed noise floor for an AUC quantity = max - min over the 5 seeds of the reference model's AUC.
  - Paired bootstrap on the seed-20261125 test set, B = 200 resamples (rng seed 20261125),
    percentile 95% CI for AUC differences.
  - Proportions within a group (e.g. sensitivity among prediabetes respondents): Wilson 95% CI,
    reported per seed.
  - Logistic-regression odds ratios (Q3): Wald 95% CI from statsmodels Logit on the full file.
Numbers truncated (not rounded) to 4 decimals.

General AUC materiality rule (used wherever "AUC rule" is cited below):
  An AUC difference D (paired, same split) is MATERIAL if all three hold:
   (a) |median_5seeds(D)| >= 0.01;
   (b) |median_5seeds(D)| > seed noise floor of the reference model's AUC;
   (c) paired bootstrap 95% CI of D on the seed-20261125 test set excludes 0.
  NOT MATERIAL if (a) fails and the bootstrap CI lies inside (-0.01, 0.01).
  Otherwise INCONCLUSIVE.

======================================================================
Q1  DIABETES FILE (BRFSS 2015, Kaggle diabetes_012, 253,680 rows)
======================================================================
Labels: ACTUAL y_A = (Diabetes_012 == 2) [this is Diabetes_binary as distributed];
        DOCUMENTED y_D = (Diabetes_012 in {1,2}) ["prediabetes or diabetes"].
Features: the 21 columns other than the label (same as the binary file).
Fits per seed: LR_A, LGBM_A (on y_A); LR_D, LGBM_D (on y_D). 4 fits x 5 seeds.

Q1a (prediabetes respondents under the actual label). For LR_A and LGBM_A at t1 and t2:
  sensitivity among test respondents with Diabetes_012 == 1 (prediabetes, ~1,389 per test set),
  among Diabetes_012 == 2 (diabetes), and the false-positive share among Diabetes_012 == 0;
  plus the sensitivity a documentation-trusting reader would compute against y_D,
  Sens_D(model_A) = flagged among {1,2} / n{1,2}, next to Sens_A(model_A) = flagged among {2} / n{2}.
  Criterion MATERIAL if BOTH:
   (i) for both models at both thresholds, in all 5 seeds, the Wilson upper bound of sensitivity among
       prediabetes respondents is below the Wilson lower bound of sensitivity among diabetes respondents;
   (ii) Sens_A(model_A) - Sens_D(model_A) >= 0.02 for both models at t2 in all 5 seeds.
  NOT MATERIAL if neither holds; INCONCLUSIVE if only one holds.

Q1b (documented vs actual label: AUC). Report AUC(model_A on y_A), AUC(model_D on y_D), and the
  cross-evaluations AUC(model_A on y_D), AUC(model_D on y_A). Primary contrast per family:
  D1 = AUC(model_D on y_D) - AUC(model_A on y_A) (what a reader would report under each label).
  Reference model for noise floor: model_A on y_A. Verdict by the AUC rule (either family material
  -> MATERIAL; both not material -> NOT MATERIAL).

Q1c (calibration-in-the-large). CITL of model_A against y_D (what a reader trusting the documented
  label would observe) and against y_A; same for model_D. Also mean predicted risk of model_A vs
  model_D. Criterion MATERIAL if |CITL(model_A vs y_D)| >= 0.01 in all 5 seeds for both families.
  (Note pre-stated: this is close to arithmetic, since 4,631/253,680 = 0.0182; reported for
  completeness, not as an independent finding.)

Q1d (feature ranking). Permutation importance for model_A (on y_A) and model_D (on y_D), same seed.
  Within-label reproducibility floor: top-10 overlap between model_A of seed k and model_A of seed
  k+1 (k = 1..4), minimum over pairs.
  Criterion MATERIAL (per family) if median over seeds of top-10 overlap(A, D) < 7 AND below the
  within-label minimum. NOT MATERIAL if median overlap >= 8 or >= the within-label minimum.
  Otherwise INCONCLUSIVE. Kendall tau-b reported descriptively. Overall: material if either family.

======================================================================
Q2  HEART FILE (BRFSS 2022, Kaggle heart_2022_no_nans, 246,022 rows)
======================================================================
Fits per seed (both families):
  M1: HadHeartAttack ~ all 39 other columns (includes HadAngina) [as distributed].
  M2: HadHeartAttack ~ 38 columns (HadAngina removed).
  M3: y20 = HadHeartAttack OR HadAngina (the 2020 MI-or-CHD definition, as reconstructed in the
      manuscript: 21,616 positives) ~ 37 columns (HadHeartAttack and HadAngina removed).
Q2a (angina co-feature). D2 = AUC(M1) - AUC(M2) on HadHeartAttack, reference model M2.
  Plus: HadAngina rank and share of summed positive permutation importance in M1.
  Criterion MATERIAL if the AUC rule holds for D2 in either family, OR HadAngina is ranked 1st in
  >= 4/5 seeds with share >= 0.25 (median) in either family. NOT MATERIAL if the AUC rule gives NOT
  MATERIAL for both families and HadAngina is not ranked 1st in any seed. Otherwise INCONCLUSIVE.
Q2b (label drift 2020 vs 2022 definition). Prevalence of y20 vs HadHeartAttack in the file;
  D3 = AUC(M3 on y20) - AUC(M2 on HadHeartAttack), reference M2. Cross-evaluation:
  AUC(M2 on y20), AUC(M3 on HadHeartAttack). Verdict by the AUC rule on D3 (either family).
  Prevalence change reported (0.0546 vs 0.0878 expected) without a threshold (it is arithmetic).
Descriptive only (no verdict): LR and LGBM on heart_2020_cleaned (HeartDisease ~ 17 columns),
  5 seeds, AUC, to show what a user comparing "heart disease" AUC across versions sees.

Q3  ALCOHOL VARIABLE (heart 2020 AlcoholDrinking vs heart 2022 AlcoholDrinkers)
======================================================================
Full files, unweighted, statsmodels Logit:
  2020: HeartDisease ~ AlcoholDrinking (crude); + AgeCategory (categorical, reference = youngest)
        + Sex (adjusted).
  2022 (heart_2022_no_nans): HadHeartAttack ~ AlcoholDrinkers (crude; adjusted as above).
  Secondary 2022: y20 ~ AlcoholDrinkers (crude; adjusted), to separate label change from
        variable change.
Report: Yes-prevalence of each alcohol variable, outcome rate by Yes/No, OR with Wald 95% CI.
Criterion MATERIAL if (i) the adjusted OR 95% CIs of 2020 and 2022 (primary outcomes) do not
  overlap, OR (ii) within one version, crude and adjusted ORs lie on opposite sides of 1 with both
  CIs excluding 1. NOT MATERIAL if the adjusted CIs overlap and no sign change occurs.
  Stated as descriptive associations, not causal effects.
