VACC_H1N1_F / VACC_SEAS_F; INT_MONTH taken from the linked NHFS row. Features: the 35 distributed features as coded.
Models, splits, materiality as P9 Section 3.9: logistic regression (C=1, numeric standardized after median imputation

   numeric with 3 set missing (imputed, with a "don't know" indicator), each target. P9 rule.

   health_insurance and doctor_recc_h1n1 only; H1N1 vaccinated share by month; weighted (FLUWT) vs unweighted shares.
